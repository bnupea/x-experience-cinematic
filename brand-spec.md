## Visual system

- Palette: tobacco `#1A0F0C`, wine `#4A201A`, cream `#EAD8C2`, ivory `#F7EFE4`, brass `#D9AD64`, rust `#A23F2F`
- Display: Cormorant Garamond 500/600
- Interface: Manrope 400/500/600
- Geometry: square image fields, no glass cards, pills or ornamental component chrome
- Grid: 12 columns desktop, linear full-width scenes at 320–760px
- Motion: native scroll, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, no pinned sections, reduced-motion static path

## Image rules

- The physical device remains legible and dominant at every viewport.
- Desktop collection scenes may be environmental; mobile scenes must prioritise the complete device silhouette.
- Generated visuals communicate atmosphere only and must not be interpreted as dimensional or technical product documentation.
- No stock photography, fabricated partner logos, fake metrics or CSS-built device silhouettes.
